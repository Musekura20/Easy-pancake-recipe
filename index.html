My favorite Pancake Recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 egg
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • A pinch of salt

Instructions

  1. Mix the dry ingredients.
  2. Add the egg and milk.
  3. Mix until smooth.
  4. Heat a pan.
  5. Cook each pancake until golden.
  6. Serve and enjoy.
